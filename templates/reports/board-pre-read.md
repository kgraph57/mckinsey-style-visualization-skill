## Executive summary

Growth is on plan and retention is healthy, but onboarding time has drifted past target for two consecutive quarters. This pre-read asks the board to approve a one-quarter onboarding investment rather than wait for the metric to resolve on its own.

## Performance against plan

Every headline metric except onboarding time is within range this quarter. Onboarding time is the one item that has moved from a management update to a board decision.

![KPI performance against target](spec:specs/kpi-scorecard.json)

Onboarding time has moved from 14 to 19 days over two quarters while every other metric held or improved, which is why it is called out on its own rather than folded into the general commentary.

## The ARR bridge

New-logo growth remains the primary driver of ARR and continues to outpace churn, even as churn widens.

![ARR bridge, Q1 to Q4](spec:specs/arr-waterfall.json)

Churn is now the largest single negative driver on the bridge. It has not yet slowed new-logo growth, but the two trends are on a collision course if onboarding time keeps drifting.

## Risks to flag

- **Onboarding time** is the leading indicator management is most confident in; it moves before retention does.
  - Two consecutive quarters of drift is the threshold that triggered this pre-read.
- **Support headcount** is flat against a growing account base.
- **Renewal concentration**: the top ten accounts represent a disproportionate share of ARR at risk if onboarding does not improve before their renewal dates.

## The ask

The board is asked to approve a one-quarter onboarding investment, funded from the existing operating budget, with a review at the next board meeting against the same scorecard shown above.
